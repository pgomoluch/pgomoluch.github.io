Paweł Gomoluch

Hi, I'm Paweł. I'm a computer scientist working on AI and drug discovery at DaltonTx. Previously, I worked at BenevolentAI and GSK. My research interests include molecular property prediction, modeling interactions between proteins and small molecules, and active learning.

I received my PhD from the Department of Computing, Imperial College London. My thesis was focused on enhancing model-based combinatorial search using machine learning techniques.
